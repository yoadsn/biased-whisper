> **Smoke-level evidence only.** One model (`yoad/whisper-small`), one domain (Knesset speech), small N, no repeats.
> It shows the pipeline works and gives a first signal; it does not confirm the hypothesis.

# Setup

* Corpus: 146 utterances (6-25 s) built from segments with digits; all from `yoad/knesset-*` in the local HF cache.
* Phase 1 on all 146 (two runs), model `yoad/whisper-small`, greedy, no timestamps, MPS.
* Phase 2: 73 decision points (verdict `cardinal`), `param_filter=bitfit` (271,872 knobs: biases + LayerNorm),
  `objective=mass`, `nudge_mode=linear`, `normalize=rms`, `sample_pct=5`, `common_pct=30`, `agree_top_pct=50`, group size 3,
  1500 rounds, `fdr=0.05`.
* Phase 3: 40 held-out utterances (not Phase 2 points), strengths 2/5/10/20, controls `shuffled_values` and `random_indices`.

# Phase 1 - the model knows both forms

Of 146 samples about 60% produced digits in the free decode. For the first 40: 25 `cardinal`, 3 `partial`, 8 `degenerate`,
4 already spelled out. Examples (digits banned from the decision point):

| free decode | digits banned |
|---|---|
| בשנת 2020 | בשנת אלפיים עשרים |
| 17 דקות | שבע עשרה דקות |
| 61 בעד לעומת 50 נגד ו-9 נמנעים | שישים ואחת בעד לעומת חמישים נגד ותשעה נמנעים |

* Mean digit probability mass at the decision step: 0.88-0.91 (the model is usually confident in digits but the cardinal
  form is always the runner-up family).
* **Failure mode:** ~22% of digit samples are `degenerate`; most have the hyphen-prefix token (`ב-`) right before the digits.
  Banning digits then yields `ב-ב-ב-...`. See [Review](/theory/review-of-approach.md).

# Phase 2 - structure of the nudge

* Gradient energy is concentrated in the **decoder (~80%)**, specifically residual-write biases of layers 4-5
  (`self_attn.out_proj.bias`, `encoder_attn.out_proj.bias`, `fc2.bias`) - i.e. additive steering directions.
* Vote distribution has a clear heavy tail over the permutation null (Gini 0.23-0.24 real vs 0.11-0.12 null); split-half
  Jaccard@1% = 0.80-0.82.
* At `fdr=0.05` the thresholds select **6,120 (`to_digits`) and 6,892 (`to_cardinal`)** knobs out of 271,872 - a distributed set,
  not a handful. Encoder `fc1.bias` entries are numerous among candidates even though decoder knobs carry more energy.
* **The two nudge types are near-mirrors:** 93% of `to_digits` candidates are also `to_cardinal` candidates, and 90% of those
  shared knobs have opposite sign. This is expected (same gradient, opposite sign) and means agreement is not validation.

# Phase 3 - control experiments (held-out, n = 40 utterances)

Digit share of number mentions (baseline 44.2%, 42% of outputs contain a digit):

| strength | `to_digits` | `to_cardinal` | shuffled control | random-index control |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2  | 44.2% | 43.4% | 44.2% | 44.2% |
| 5  | 48.1% | 38.9% | 44.2% | 44.2% |
| 10 | 55.6% | 27.5% | 44.2% | 44.2% |
| 20 | 59.6% | **17.6%** | 46.2% | 44.2% |

* Dose-dependent, **bidirectional**, and both controls are flat. Probe p(digit) at the decision points moves 0.88 -> 0.98 (`to_digits`)
  and 0.88 -> 0.08 (`to_cardinal` x20), as the linear model predicted in direction.
* **Cost:** `text_drift` (change in non-number words vs baseline) grows with strength: 0.04 at x10, 0.08-0.16 at x20 for
  `to_cardinal`. No degenerate outputs at any strength. A usable operating point is probably around x5-x10 - needs a proper
  quality metric (WER after number normalisation) before concluding.

# Caveats and next steps

1. N is tiny (~50 number mentions per condition); get >= 200 eval utterances, repeat across seeds/splits, add confidence intervals.
2. Same domain for fit and eval; test on `crowd-recital` / other ivrit.ai sources and the reverse-style source.
3. Effect is spread over thousands of bias knobs - consistent with a residual steering direction, not a tiny neuron set. Test a
   single-layer steering vector and neuron-level activation attribution next.
4. Add trivial baselines (digit-token logit bias, prompting) before claiming the knobs are interesting.
5. Compare `normalize`, `objective`, `nudge_mode`, and `param_filter` (`decoder_no_embed` is the next step up).
