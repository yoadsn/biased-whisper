Phase 1 — does the model know both forms?

Decode freely; at the first digit token, rewind, ban every digit-related token and continue. A cardinal verdict means the replaced span became number words and the rest of the sentence stayed intact. Review verdicts by hand: good/bad override the automatic one and decide which points feed phase 2.

Phase 2 — discover upstream knobs

For each verified decision point: nudge the digit-vs-cardinal log-odds to the target probability, keep the top-% of the nudge, take the commonly selected params, then vote across random groups and keep what survives a permutation-null FDR threshold.
